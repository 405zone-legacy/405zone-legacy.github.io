Hazed Plains

DESCRIPTION

WARNING: HIGHLY TOXIC AND FLAMMABLE AREA!

It is recommended that you wear appropriate equipment before entering this area.

It is a biome composed of red hues, with a dense toxic fog (especially for animals) surrounding it. All vegetation and species within this place are poisoned or dead. Although the fog is not toxic to humans, it exposes them to many hallucinations, making them more prone if they enter without the appropriate equipment. A curious detail is that the fog in this biome is capable of confusing most hostile beings and even causing them to lose sight of you.

ORIGIN

Hazed Plains is the result of a large accumulation of stray spores from the valley that, after oscillating between life and death over time, managed to develop the first "Hazed Plains". Thanks to the ruins we find here, we can learn about their civilization. We have concluded that there were two types of people within the legacy of these areas: those who arrived over time, of whom we have found some camps, and those who inhabited that area. Thanks to a historian, we know that the latter gradually lost their sanity due to the confusion caused by the toxic fog.

It was a generation completely destined for extinction; they lost their coherence, contradicted and opposed each other, manipulated each other without scruples, became completely sadistic, and killed each other in the worst possible ways. They completely lost control, and all their decisions were dictated by a creature they idolized, an unreal creature visible to them due to hallucinations caused by the high dose of spores inhaled from that plain. Anyone who opposed Rottenmorphen (or whom they interpreted as such, since they had no conscience) was to be banished or annihilated; they nicknamed him, referring to him as a "rotten (corrupt) mass," most likely associated with the number of people who were murdered in his name.

VEGETATION

The biome is completely devastated in terms of vegetation. In this case, we can assure you that every plant is designed to be a deadly trap. This biome has its own moss, which comes from the "Corrupted Moss" subjected to the high temperatures of this biome; even so, it still maintains the "Corrupted Grass" and the 'Corruptles' of the "Ominous Valley."

However, this biome has a rather peculiar flora, in that it does not grow naturally but rather infects it.

For example, a "Necroveil," which was used as a funeral plant, becomes a "Flaming Hope," as if somehow it persists or does not lose hope. Ironically, they seem to be their antithesis.

Another similar case occurs with the "Smiley Bud", which is quite rare, as we only know that this plant laughs and has no other particular function. Here it transforms into a "One-Motem", which is covered in thorns and can be lethal. One theory about this plant is that it is believed that it could grow someday, but that it may be a very slow process.

Finally, we have the "Fumackia Ignervosas," "Deathuras," and "Cherossom," which are the only fruitful food sources in this area.

Still, keep in mind that "Agitated Fruit" -- and especially "Toxinapple" and "Nulled Cherries" -- may seem harmless at first glance but can actually be lethal.

It should be noted that "Corruptinies" can be found within this habitat, where they are considered intruders or even pests. Unlike in "Ominous Valley," here they are prone to spreading their spores and expanding their territory, as if it were a plant war.

RESEARCH ADVANCES

As we already know, this is an uninhabitable area for vegetation, humans, and especially animals; this is because the fog is toxic to them but not lethal, leaving them completely defenseless. Something curious is that when they die, they lose all their value; you can't get anything out of them except this green meat that our scientists have decided to nickname "Strangely Familiar Meat".

KNOWN THREATS

CURSED MADNESS

It is well known that fog is an important factor in this environment for protecting yourself from hostile creatures after getting lost in it; however, it only takes a warning call to give away anyone hiding behind it. The "Cursed Madness" (a nickname given by our researchers) are lost 'Soulbound' from the "Ominous Valley" who managed to survive and adapt to their environment despite its deterioration. Their goal is not to hunt or devour you like other entities; they are simply there to alert everyone nearby to your position for a while.

Due to their weakness, they prefer to help others do the dirty work while they wander around after completing their suicide mission. Due to their deterioration, they often have melted metal pieces to try to hold their bodies together, they move by bouncing due to their missing limbs, they lose eyes, their horns become charred, etc. But they all have something in common: they use a pale pumpkin as a defense system to confuse potential predators. Fun fact: if it is killed, it will explode due to the accumulation of spores it has consumed over time; there is also the possibility that its pumpkin will light up once it dies, leaving us with an "Ominous Pumpkin," because its soul is now trapped in that pumpkin.

ROTTENMORPHEN

This entity is not real; it is a hallucination caused by the spores of "Hazed Plains." Simply inhaling them exposes you to a huge number of hallucinations, the most prominent of which is this one. Even if you have not interacted with an area like this before, you are still in danger, because, as mentioned in Global Impact, the spores can be found everywhere. Rottenmorphen is the name given to it. Its goal is to feed on the fear of its victims, so it plays on their fears to keep them at bay, because as long as they are tense, it has more opportunities to attack.

Its physical appearance is a kind of amalgam of several things: a ram, bloody tentacles for legs, exposed guts on its underside, dark blue horns, and a dense amount of noise as if it were rotting. It has an empty gaze, and its facial features look as if they have been torn apart by hand. Its face is split in half both horizontally and vertically, and between the cracks you can find various fangs twisted in various directions, which it uses to devour the fears of its prey. Despite this, it is not lethal; it is almost impossible for it to kill you. You could only suffer a heart attack, and that's it.
