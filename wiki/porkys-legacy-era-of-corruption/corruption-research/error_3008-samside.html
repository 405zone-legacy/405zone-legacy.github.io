DESCRIPTION

The original myth of this area describes it as an endless forest filled with trees that reach up to the clouds, deep darkness, a dense fog that hides its intentions, and a confusingly calming tranquility. It is considered a hell that attempts to replicate reality, a home for all those who know the truth. They say that everyone who comes here never returns, that everything there works backwards; fear is your alarm, and you must never let your guard down, because once you are vulnerable, your cries will only be heard by entities of insatiable hunger.

H I M

This being is a myth, considered by many to be a source of hope and by others a judge, according to the prophecy: "The Void Walker." A four-legged creature that seems to have total control over the legacy of the universe, a manipulator and destroyer. It has only one goal: to take with it anyone who has seen too much, and because of this, there is no proof of its existence. For now, it is all writings and theories; without solid proof, this is all we can say. Honestly, for us, it is a simple excuse to blame for all corruption, just by categorizing it as the leader of a fictional land.
