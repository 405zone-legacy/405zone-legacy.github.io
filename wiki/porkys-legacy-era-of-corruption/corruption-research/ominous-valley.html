Ominous Valley

>> Temperature: >MOSTLY_COLD;
>> ClimateDownfall: >OCCASIONALLY;
>> Civilization: >CONSUMED;
>> DangerLevel: >2.75/5;

DESCRIPTION

It is a completely desolate, mystical valley, composed of pale and dark purple colors. It has vegetation known as "corruption"; every plant within this biome is designed to be a trap for its prey. A dense fog created from spores is one of the biggest obstacles within this area. All vegetation, including trees, shares a special quality: during the day they are weak and enter a kind of "sleep mode," but when night falls they become resistant. Some evidence of this is how the fog becomes denser at nightfall or how hard it becomes to even hit a tree trunk in this biome.

ORIGIN

There is no clear explanation for why they exist or why there are so many of them, but thanks to the in-depth research of our scientists, we have discovered that once tree roots touch the ground, they begin to dig at a rapid pace and resurface to expand their territory.

The thing is, this isn't always the case, because if one of them fails to expand its territory, it will flourish in its place as a "Corruptiny," treating it as if it were some kind of starting point.

Within the valley, we have also found several structures from previous civilizations, but this has not been very helpful either. What we can confirm is that this valley is designed to trap its victims, as it is capable of recreating the voices of deceased beings in order to attract them. This confirms that the large number of disappearances within this valley were not mere suicides, but manipulations of unreal voices.

VEGETATION

This is the area with the most vegetation. The initial base focuses on its "Corrupted Moss," which is usually accompanied by "Corrupted Grass," which are simple, harmless little weeds; "Corruptles," a more developed version of the previous one with the ability to entangle and slow down its prey in order to make them more accessible to the possible dangers of the valley; and "Ghostflow Bushes," which are simply shrubs that, at nightfall, tend to release a kind of mixture between liquid and gas that evaporates instantly and seems to take the shape of sorrowful faces.

As we mentioned earlier, trees have amazing qualities, but especially their leaves; these are the cause of the roots, which our scientists have named "Root of Corruption," referring to the fact that they are the cause of the problem. Returning to the subject of leaves, they are likely to have "Ominous Saplings" so that these trees can grow in other places. It should be noted that in some very isolated cases, they can grow without leaves, as if they were completely dead.

Finally, in relation to trees, we have discovered that both the leaves and roots bear fruit. In the case of the roots, we have "Corrupted Berries," which are a healthy and delicious resource. However, in the case of leaves, there is a chance that they may contain a "Sinner Apple." This strange apple can satisfy your hunger in an instant, but you will still feel hungry. Not only that, but it will also add a kind of curse or something similar that will alert all kinds of entities that may be near you, leaving you completely exposed to any danger.

Something curious is that this valley has some mushrooms that are both curious and useful. These are "glowshrooms," which are very useful. If you press their stems, they shoot their spores in all directions, giving you the ability to see the silhouette of whatever is hiding behind the fog. The wonderful thing about this is that their spores regenerate after a while, which has helped us explore more of the valley with greater ease. Fun fact: if planted in mycelium and fertilized, they can grow to become giant, which is the only way to turn them into a renewable resource.

Finally, before discussing the flowers of the valley, it should be noted that the "Corruptiny" can be found within the valley; however, they will not spread their spores to create corrupt spores, nor will they multiply. It is as if they truly consider this to be their real home or an ally that they must not betray.

As for flowers, we have some basic ones such as "Necroveil," which seems to have been considered a funeral flower due to its apparent use in some ruins; "Empty Roses," which are simple roses that, for some strange reason, seem capable of showing symptoms of depression despite being a flower; and finally "Stalker Gloomstalk," a type of sunflower with an eye in the center, the origin and purpose of which are unknown. However, there are several theories that these flowers are there to watch you and always know where you are, as if they had some kind of connection to the threats within the valley.

Finally, in relation to the fauna, we have two flowers that do not seem to be normal and commonplace... We have the "Bonesay," which are deformed or twisted bones with "Corrupted Moss" on top to match the leaves, suggesting that the victims of the valley end up turning into flowers or are simply used as fertilizer. And finally, we have the "Smiley Bud," a type of flower with a smile that occasionally laughs. This flower does not seem to serve any other purpose. Once we learned this, we discovered that those pale pumpkins with purple lighting were not simply decorative, but that the light was actually some souls trapped inside them.

RESEARCH ADVANCES

It has been discovered that inside the valley (and in some cases outside it), purple barrels locked with padlocks have been found. They tried everything to open them, but nothing seemed to work. Finally, it was discovered that these can only be opened with "Hornbranch." After opening it, it dropped four objects and exploded. However, after conducting several tests, they managed to create an improvement for the "Hornbranch," and curiously, what seemed like a simple trifle ended up becoming an improvement, especially in the contents of the barrel. Unfortunately, this improvement reduced its life capacity.

Thanks to the revival of the Ominous Pumpkins, scientists have succeeded in creating two living beings known as Corrupted Golems: iron and snow. The iron golem is disciplined, loyal, and highly protective, capable of disarming enemies and growing faster and stronger as it takes damage -- its pumpkin swelling as a visible sign of this process. In contrast, the snow golem is unstable and chaotic, leaving corrupted snow in its wake. Although physically weaker, it compensates with a wider and more erratic attack range, firing corrupted snowballs capable of inflicting severe negative effects. It often targets its iron counterpart, seemingly out of pure annoyance.

Despite their protective nature, both golems are free souls and do not tolerate being attacked; if they feel threatened, they will not hesitate to kill anyone, including their owner. For safety reasons, it is strongly advised to always carry scissors or a similar tool: the pumpkin is the core of their existence, and removing it strips them of all purpose, leaving them as empty vessels.

KNOWN THREATS

THE SOULBOUND

There is only one danger in the valley: the "Soulbound," statues made of the same wood as the fence. Because of this, they share the same characteristics as the fence. For example, "During the day, they go into hibernation and are vulnerable, but when night falls, they become resistant and active." However, it has been discovered that they are only able to move when no one is watching them. This is their strategy to deceive their victims into thinking they are mere statues.

They are capable of persuading, climbing great distances without attracting attention, and even moving at great speed. They were given their name because of their ability to use the voices of loved ones to lure their prey.
